# X Image Heuristics

Use these heuristics to choose a social-image strategy. Pick one primary strategy and keep the prompt coherent.

## Gaze Hook

Use when the subject is a person, character, animal, mask, statue, or face-like object.

- Make the eyes or face the first read at thumbnail size.
- Keep the face crisp and the background softer.
- Use a small highlight or color accent near the eyes.
- Avoid crowded faces, duplicated figures, or overly busy foregrounds.

## World Gap

Use when the image can imply a larger story.

- Add one unexplained but readable object, portal, note, light fragment, ritual tool, strange weather cue, or distant structure.
- Make the story gap visible but not literal.
- Leave enough negative space for viewers to imagine context.
- Avoid text that explains the mystery.

## Quiet Emotion

Use when the attached reference is soft, calm, melancholic, nostalgic, or elegant.

- Use restrained expressions and balanced negative space.
- Favor pale highlights, soft fog, gentle grain, and low-noise compositions.
- Put the emotional cue in posture, gaze, distance, or light.
- Avoid exaggerated reaction poses.

## Color Snap

Use when the reference folder has a mostly muted palette with a few vivid accents.

- Use one sharp accent near the focal area.
- Keep the rest of the palette controlled.
- Let the accent create immediate thumbnail contrast.
- Avoid many competing saturated colors.

## Motion Freeze

Use when the request mentions drones, surprise shots, speed, wind, falling objects, water, smoke, or sudden moments.

- Freeze the focal subject while blurring foreground or atmosphere.
- Use diagonal motion lines, flying debris, hair movement, or displaced mist.
- Keep the silhouette readable.
- Avoid losing the focal face or object to motion blur.

## Default X Format

- Use vertical 4:5 unless the user specifies a different ratio.
- Make the image readable on a phone at small size.
- Use no in-image text unless the user supplies exact text.
- Favor a single strong subject, clear silhouette, and one quote-worthy mystery.
- Optimize for stopping power and emotional residue, not literal engagement bait.
